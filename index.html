    Our story

    A café built on small joys, one layer at a time.

    Mirthy was started by two founders who share one simple belief: a good pastry can turn an ordinary hour into something worth remembering. Together they set out to build a place that feels warm and unhurried, where the cake is made with care and nobody is rushed to finish it.

    The name says it all. Mirth means joy, and every layer, whether cream, sponge, flavour or conversation, is there to add a little more of it. We keep our batches small, our recipes honest and our doors open to everyone, because the best part of any dessert is who you share it with.

    Every layer is a little more joy.
